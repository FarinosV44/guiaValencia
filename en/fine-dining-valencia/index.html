Pay big and step up a level. Make sure it’s the restaurant’s level

Paying a lot can buy technique, produce, service and a different experience. It does not buy an obligation to leave thrilled. Here we explain who it makes sense for and what can spoil a special occasion.

A deliberate food occasion with time to spare: choose how long you want to sit, too.

When the spend is worth it

Book the format you want, know the cost of drinks and the cancellation terms, and flag restrictions in advance. If what you want is plenty of rice and an open-ended lunch, a good rice restaurant may suit you better.

For an occasion chosen with care, not a whim: book, arrive hungry and unhurried. ‘Bargain’, ‘rip-off’ or ‘the best’ are labels you only earn by eating. Forcing a ‘but’ for every star would be another pamphlet, just an angry one.
