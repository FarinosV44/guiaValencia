Eating cheaply in Valencia: what you pay on the way out, not what the board says

Cheap isn’t the smallest number on the menu: it’s what you pay on the way out. A €10 pizza with two drinks and dessert is no longer a €10 pizza. Here we tell you what each price includes.

Esmorzar: the cheapest and most Valencian plan

A sandwich, peanuts, olives, a drink and coffee mid-morning. Cassalla and La Pascuala have a full esmorzar at a set price; at Cremaet and Central Bar the sandwich comes on its own.

Bar Cassalla

esmorzar bar, Abastos / Arrancapins

Documentary research

A first taste of esmorzar opposite the Mercado de Abastos, with a charcoal grill in full view.

The catch: Booking: that service is walk-in. Nor a beach plan: it is in Abastos.

€8.50 per person, partial total

La Pascuala

esmorzar bodega, Malvarrosa / seafront promenade

Documentary researchGood value

The old-school bodega esmorzar, before a stroll round Malvarrosa or Cabanyal.

The catch: Sundays: closed according to both website and menu. Nor for following it with a huge paella out of habit.

€14 per person, partial total

Bar Cremaet

esmorzar bar, Avenida del Puerto

Documentary research

Esmorzar on a terrace facing the Casa del Médico, finished with the house cremaet.

The catch: A beach plan: it is at the start of Avenida del Puerto, not on the seafront.

€6 per person, base price

Central Bar

bar, Central Market / Sant Francesc

Documentary research

Ricard Camarena’s sandwich at the Mercado Central counter: a market esmorzar or tapas at lunchtime.

The catch: Dinner: the kitchen closes at 15:00 (15:30 on Friday and Saturday).

€7.50 per person, partial total

Esmorzar: Valencia’s substantial mid-morning ritual

Set lunch menus

The weekday lunch menu is still the city’s best value. Malkebien (reviews mention about €16 with everything included), Gato Blanco (with a play area) and La Negri.

Malkebien

restaurant, Torrefiel

Documentary researchGood value

A weekday lunch in Torrefiel: a home-style set menu with lots of vegetables, quick and good value, with a loyal local crowd.

The catch: Dinner: its opening table ends at 17:00, even though the website promotes dinners. Call before going in the evening.

Gato Blanco

family restaurant, Zaidía / Trinitat / Viveros

Documentary research

Families who want to eat while the children play in the same place, with a gluten-free kitchen.

The catch: Mondays and Tuesdays: closed.

€15.50 per person, partial total

La Negri

bar-restaurant, Benimaclet

Documentary research

Globe-trotting neighbourhood cooking in Benimaclet, with a cheesecake that has a reputation, and a terrace.

The catch: Mondays and Tuesdays: closed according to its website.

€15 per person, base price

Pizza and burgers

Km0 and Filippa’s have pizzas under €12; Tasty, a smash burger with fries. Add a drink: that’s where the difference lies.

Pizzería Km0

pizzeria, Benimaclet

Documentary researchGood value

Neapolitan pizza on 48-hour dough with local produce, and the best vegan section on our list, in Benimaclet.

The catch: Anyone who only wants the usual Margherita: the seasonal pizzas are the point here.

€10.50 per person, base price

Filippa’s

pizzeria, Ruzafa

Documentary research

A table of mixed tastes in Ruzafa: Neapolitan pizza for some, pasta for others.

The catch: Strict coeliacs: having gluten-free options is not the same as a trace-free kitchen. Ask about their protocol first.

€11.50 per person, base price

Tasty El Carmen

burger restaurant, El Carmen / La Seu

Documentary research

A cheap, casual smash burger in El Carmen: reviews rank it among the best smash burgers in Valencia.

The catch: A play area: a children's menu does not make it one.

€12.50 per person, partial total

Lunchtime buffet

Aoyama on weekday lunchtimes, dessert included. Compulsory drinks extra and €2 for each plate you leave: order little and go back for more.

Aoyama

sushi buffet, Eixample / Colón / Cánovas

Documentary researchA bargain, with conditions

A lunchtime Japanese buffet for those who come for the fish rather than the sauces; dessert is included.

The catch: Light eaters: compare with an à la carte Japanese restaurant first.

€18 per person, partial total
