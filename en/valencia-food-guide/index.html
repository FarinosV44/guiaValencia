Valencia, from pizza comfort to full local immersion

You are allowed to land in Valencia and want pizza. Your passport will survive. But if you fancy going further, this guide helps you understand what is on the plate, what the bill covers and whether the plan suits you.

Comfort zone: “My passport still smells of mozzarella”

Italian food and burgers. Familiar food can be a perfectly good choice; it doesn’t make somewhere a tourist trap, or local and authentic.

Filippa’s

pizzeria, Ruzafa

Documentary research

A table of mixed tastes in Ruzafa: Neapolitan pizza for some, pasta for others.

The catch: Strict coeliacs: having gluten-free options is not the same as a trace-free kitchen. Ask about their protocol first.

€11.50 per person, base price

Raíces

pizzeria, El Carmen / La Seu

Documentary research

Contemporary pizza from a short menu by the Cathedral, for groups of two to four who don’t mind waiting for a table.

The catch: Needing a guaranteed seating time: no bookings.

€19 per person, partial total

Pizzería Km0

pizzeria, Benimaclet

Documentary researchGood value

Neapolitan pizza on 48-hour dough with local produce, and the best vegan section on our list, in Benimaclet.

The catch: Anyone who only wants the usual Margherita: the seasonal pizzas are the point here.

€10.50 per person, base price

Tasty El Carmen

burger restaurant, El Carmen / La Seu

Documentary research

A cheap, casual smash burger in El Carmen: reviews rank it among the best smash burgers in Valencia.

The catch: A play area: a children's menu does not make it one.

€12.50 per person, partial total

First local steps: “One foot outside the airport lounge”

Try tortilla, croquetas or esgarraet: roasted peppers and salted cod, with the exact recipe checked at the venue. Rice is a whole category: ask whether the price is per person, whether there is a minimum for two, and whether you need to order ahead.

Bar Ricardo

bar, La Petxina / Extramurs

Documentary research

Classic neighbourhood tapas with serious produce in La Petxina: bravas, ensaladilla and cuttlefish.

The catch: Ordering seafood by weight without asking: the bill moves into another league.

€15.20 per person, partial total

Central Bar

bar, Central Market / Sant Francesc

Documentary research

Ricard Camarena’s sandwich at the Mercado Central counter: a market esmorzar or tapas at lunchtime.

The catch: Dinner: the kitchen closes at 15:00 (15:30 on Friday and Saturday).

€7.50 per person, partial total

La Pepica

rice restaurant, Malvarrosa / seafront promenade

Documentary research

Rice, a stroll and ice cream along the same Neptuno promenade.

The catch: Anyone in a hurry: the venue itself warns of at least 40–45 minutes for rice, longer at peak times.

€30 per person, base price

Bar Cassalla

esmorzar bar, Abastos / Arrancapins

Documentary research

A first taste of esmorzar opposite the Mercado de Abastos, with a charcoal grill in full view.

The catch: Booking: that service is walk-in. Nor a beach plan: it is in Abastos.

€8.50 per person, partial total

Bar Cremaet

esmorzar bar, Avenida del Puerto

Documentary research

Esmorzar on a terrace facing the Casa del Médico, finished with the house cremaet.

The catch: A beach plan: it is at the start of Avenida del Puerto, not on the seafront.

€6 per person, base price

Go deeper: “Tell me what it is before I become adventurous”

Eel, tripe, blood dishes and horse meat are not euphemisms we hide in a translation. We name ingredients when a dish actually contains them. Ask about the recipe of a figatell rather than assuming every version is the same. You can enjoy local food without ordering any of these.

Anyora

bodega restaurant, Cabanyal / Canyamelar

Documentary research

Valencian food chosen for culinary reasons, not just the décor.

The catch: Sundays: the kitchen is closed according to its website.

€19.50 per person, partial total

Creative local cooking: “A bigger bill deserves better questions”

La Salita

fine dining, Ruzafa

Documentary research

A deliberate food occasion with time to spare: choose how long you want to sit, too.

The catch: Choosing each dish or a quick dinner. With a child who needs to move, three hours of tasting menu feel long, children’s menu or not.

€128.70 per person, partial total

RiFF

fine dining, Eixample / Colón / Cánovas

Documentary research

A special occasion built on local produce; ask for the counter if you like watching the kitchen work.

The catch: Anyone who needs natural light or a specific table: ask for it when you book.

€89.80 per person, partial total

Pay big and step up a level. Make sure it’s the restaurant’s level

Small questions, useful answers

English copy is not evidence that staff speak English.

¿Hay medio bocadillo?Is a half sandwich available?
¿El arroz es por persona? ¿Mínimo dos?Is rice priced per person, with a two-person minimum?
¿Cuánto cuesta lo que está fuera de carta?How much is the special?
¿Podemos pedir sin móvil?Can we order without using a phone?
