How we are funded

No restaurant pays us at the moment.

We work for the people choosing where to eat. If a venue pays for or hosts a visit, we will tell you: who set the table, who paid for the visit and what our relationship is.

We might love a cheap bar and be let down by a luxury dinner. Or the other way round. We tell you what we know, what we think and what we have not checked yet. When a venue card is based on menus and reviews, you will see it; research is not pretending we ate there.

Declared since 24 October 2026.

Our rules

  • Funding a visit does not buy an opinion, a score, a position or the removal of criticism.
  • Payment plays no part in the recommendation formula.
  • We separate fees, hosted meals, travel or accommodation, discounts, gifts and commissions, with date and venue.
  • The label sits next to the verdict, the venue card and the listing, not hidden in the footer.
  • A visit with a menu prepared because we were invited does not show normal service.
  • The restaurant may correct facts and reply; it does not approve or veto the text.
  • If we accept funding, “No restaurant pays us” is replaced by the current statement, with its history.
