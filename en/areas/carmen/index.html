A cheap, casual smash burger in El Carmen: reviews rank it among the best smash burgers in Valencia.

The catch: A play area: a children's menu does not make it one.

La Lambrusquería Caballeros

Italian restaurant

Documentary research

A casual date in El Carmen: Italian sharing plates and a tiramisu for two, with the bill under control.

The catch: A 'full date under €50' with a starter: the example leaves little room for drinks.

€22.38 per person, partial total

Blanqueries

restaurant

Documentary researchBest on the set menu

A calm, chatty lunch in El Carmen; the lunch menu is the smartest way into a restaurant listed in the Michelin Guide.

The catch: Giving an active child room to move: nothing suggests space for it.

€27 per person, partial total

La Rentaora

tavern

Documentary research

Snacks and wine on Mossén Sorell: cheese, charcuterie, then on with your stroll through El Carmen.

The catch: A full dinner when you are properly hungry: this is grazing.

Kuzina

restaurant

Documentary research

Mezze and moussaka in El Carmen, for when you want something different without ending up at an Asian buffet.

The catch: Turning up on a whim: the room is small and fills up; book.

€25.50 per person, partial total
