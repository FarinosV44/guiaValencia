Central Market / Sant Francesc

5 places in this area. Three to five per plan, not the whole catalogue.

Search this area

To eat

Hundred Burgers

burger restaurant

Documentary research

Anyone who wants to try the burger Burgerdudes named the world’s best: 100 seats and plenty of demand.

The catch: Anyone after a quiet, hidden burger joint: this is the most famous one in town.

Central Bar

bar

Documentary research

Ricard Camarena’s sandwich at the Mercado Central counter: a market esmorzar or tapas at lunchtime.

The catch: Dinner: the kitchen closes at 15:00 (15:30 on Friday and Saturday).

€7.50 per person, partial total

Linaje

family restaurant

Documentary research

Families in the centre who want to eat while the child plays on site.

The catch: Assuming supervision all day: staff for children are only declared at weekends.

For something sweet or coffee

La Romana San Vicente

ice-cream shop

Documentary research

Ice cream right on Sant Vicent, with pistachio and Italian creams you can taste first.

The catch: Anyone expecting the chain's full range: choose from what is in the cabinet.

Véneta Ribera

ice-cream shop

Documentary research

Ice cream with somewhere to sit after lunch around Sant Francesc or at Linaje.

The catch: Confusing it with the Brodadors branch.
