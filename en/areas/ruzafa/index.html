To eat

Filippa’s

pizzeria

Documentary research

A table of mixed tastes in Ruzafa: Neapolitan pizza for some, pasta for others.

The catch: Strict coeliacs: having gluten-free options is not the same as a trace-free kitchen. Ask about their protocol first.

€11.50 per person, base price

La Salita

fine dining

Documentary research

A deliberate food occasion with time to spare: choose how long you want to sit, too.

The catch: Choosing each dish or a quick dinner. With a child who needs to move, three hours of tasting menu feel long, children’s menu or not.

€128.70 per person, partial total

Canalla Bistro

restaurant

Documentary research

Globe-trotting sharing plates in Ruzafa, for couples or friends who want to try a bit of everything.

The catch: Anyone planning another dessert stop: the menu already includes desserts.

€40 per person, partial total

Nozomi Sushi Bar

restaurant

Documentary research

A Japanese counter in Ruzafa for a treat: omakase and a booking made well ahead.

The catch: Two people with €40 for everything, or a spur-of-the-moment dinner.

€70 per person, partial total

For something sweet or coffee

Antigua Lechería

ice-cream shop

Documentary research

A very Ruzafa sweet stop, with homemade ice cream in inventive flavours; Repsol recommends it.

The catch: Anyone who wants the usual ice cream: people come here for the inventive flavours.

Bluebell Coffee

café

Documentary research

Own-roasted specialty coffee in Ruzafa, in the morning or at weekend brunch.

The catch: Coffee after dinner: its website says it closes at 16:00.

Hippolatte

café with play area

Documentary research

A 3–5-year-old who needs to move; the value is the play, not the coffee.

The catch: Anyone after calm, or with a very young baby: it is designed for 3-to-5-year-olds who need to move.
