La Pascuala

esmorzar bodega

Documentary researchGood value

The old-school bodega esmorzar, before a stroll round Malvarrosa or Cabanyal.

The catch: Sundays: closed according to both website and menu. Nor for following it with a huge paella out of habit.

€14 per person, partial total

Casa Carmela

rice restaurant

Documentary research

Wood-fired rice in Malvarrosa, for a rice lunch where you don’t hold back.

The catch: A cheap meal, or an impromptu paella valenciana: it needs ordering 24 hours ahead.

€58 per person, partial total

La Pepica

rice restaurant

Documentary research

Rice, a stroll and ice cream along the same Neptuno promenade.

The catch: Anyone in a hurry: the venue itself warns of at least 40–45 minutes for rice, longer at peak times.

€30 per person, base price
