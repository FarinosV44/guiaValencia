Corrections and changes

If a price, opening time or detail on a card has changed, let us know. We review every report before changing anything: nothing is published automatically, whether it comes from a diner or the restaurant itself. Restaurants may correct facts and reply; they don’t approve or veto the text.

The sending channel isn’t active yet: prepare the report and copy it. We’ll switch the channel on once the owner sets it up.
