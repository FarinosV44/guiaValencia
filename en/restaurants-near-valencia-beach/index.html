Eating near Valencia’s beach without shrinking the map

Not all these venues are on the seafront. Choose between a morning feast and a big rice lunch rather than assuming your stomach wants both.

Esmorzar and a stroll

La Pascuala

esmorzar bodega, Malvarrosa / seafront promenade

Documentary researchGood value

The old-school bodega esmorzar, before a stroll round Malvarrosa or Cabanyal.

The catch: Sundays: closed according to both website and menu. Nor for following it with a huge paella out of habit.

€14 per person, partial total

Tapas and produce in Cabanyal

Casa Montaña

bodega restaurant, Cabanyal / Canyamelar

Documentary research

A classic Cabanyal bodega: brandada, tapas and wine, if you know what to order.

The catch: Arriving late and expecting to linger: one review mentions being rushed after arriving late.

€50 per person, partial total

Anyora

bodega restaurant, Cabanyal / Canyamelar

Documentary research

Valencian food chosen for culinary reasons, not just the décor.

The catch: Sundays: the kitchen is closed according to its website.

€19.50 per person, partial total

Cabanyal: lunch at Anyora, a stroll, then decide where dessert happens

Rice by the promenade

La Pepica and Véneta Neptuno share a promenade; Casa Carmela is a separate option further north, not next door.

La Pepica

rice restaurant, Malvarrosa / seafront promenade

Documentary research

Rice, a stroll and ice cream along the same Neptuno promenade.

The catch: Anyone in a hurry: the venue itself warns of at least 40–45 minutes for rice, longer at peak times.

€30 per person, base price

Casa Carmela

rice restaurant, Malvarrosa / seafront promenade

Documentary research

Wood-fired rice in Malvarrosa, for a rice lunch where you don’t hold back.

The catch: A cheap meal, or an impromptu paella valenciana: it needs ordering 24 hours ahead.

€58 per person, partial total

Véneta Neptuno

ice-cream shop, Malvarrosa / seafront promenade

Documentary research

Ice cream on the seafront promenade, the same one as La Pepica.

The catch: Confusing it with the Ribera branch.

Day trip: Fernandet, Sueca beach

Day trip Outside Valencia city

Outside Valencia city: a round trip, not a walk on from the Marina. Book, and call ahead out of season.

Fernandet

restaurant (day trip), Sueca beach (day trip)

Documentary researchDay trip

A rice day trip: reviews highlight the rice dishes, attentive service and a relaxed, family feel.

The catch: Breakfast: today it opens at lunchtime, not for esmorzar. Nor is it a walk from the Marina: you need a car or transport.

With a baby or an active child

A terrace is not proof of play space or supervision. None of these venues has a documented children’s area; see the families page.
