Dessert deserves a choice too: ice cream, horchata and coffee in Valencia

One scoop won’t rescue a bad lunch, but a good sweet stop can round off a good day. These are the ones with something to say: a flavour that made their name, a classic horchata or a coffee worth the walk.

House rule: if the set menu already includes dessert, ice cream outside is a treat, not part of the plan. Pick one.

Ice cream

Véneta has the awards and grandma’s biscuit; La Romana, creaminess without artificial colours and vegan options; Antigua Lechería, inventive flavours, very Ruzafa; Gelática, ice cream and brownie in Cabanyal. Ask to taste before choosing: they all welcome it.

Véneta Brodadors

ice-cream shop, El Carmen / La Seu

Documentary research

A compact sweet stop on the way to the Cathedral and Plaza de la Virgen.

The catch: Anyone who hates queues: there are some at peak times.

Véneta Ribera

ice-cream shop, Central Market / Sant Francesc

Documentary research

Ice cream with somewhere to sit after lunch around Sant Francesc or at Linaje.

The catch: Confusing it with the Brodadors branch.

Véneta Neptuno

ice-cream shop, Malvarrosa / seafront promenade

Documentary research

Ice cream on the seafront promenade, the same one as La Pepica.

The catch: Confusing it with the Ribera branch.

La Romana San Vicente

ice-cream shop, Central Market / Sant Francesc

Documentary research

Ice cream right on Sant Vicent, with pistachio and Italian creams you can taste first.

The catch: Anyone expecting the chain's full range: choose from what is in the cabinet.

La Romana Joaquín Costa

ice-cream shop, Eixample / Colón / Cánovas

Documentary research

Ice cream with seating on Joaquín Costa, near Gran Vía.

The catch: Anyone who can’t stand peak-time queues.

Antigua Lechería

ice-cream shop, Ruzafa

Documentary research

A very Ruzafa sweet stop, with homemade ice cream in inventive flavours; Repsol recommends it.

The catch: Anyone who wants the usual ice cream: people come here for the inventive flavours.

Gelática Ice Concept

ice-cream shop and patisserie, Cabanyal / Canyamelar

Documentary research

Ice cream and brownies in Cabanyal, for the afternoon or after lunch.

The catch: After dinner: it closes early and the last half hour is takeaway only.

Horchata

Horchata with fartons is the quintessential Valencian afternoon snack. Daniel serves it without leaving the Mercado de Colón; Els Sariers is a neighbourhood stop to end a stroll through Benimaclet.

Daniel Mercado de Colón

horchata bar, Eixample / Colón / Cánovas

Documentary researchGood value

A Valencian stop after lunch or for an afternoon snack: horchata and fartons without leaving the Mercado de Colón.

The catch: Ending a late dinner: it closes at 22:00 according to Mercado de Colón.

Els Sariers

horchata bar and ice-cream shop, Benimaclet

Documentary research

Classic horchata to round off a stroll through Benimaclet.

The catch: Ordering both horchata and ice cream out of duty: pick one.

Horchata
A cold, sweet drink made from tiger nuts.
Fartons
Long glazed buns for dipping in horchata.
Flotador
Horchata with a scoop of ice cream floating in it.
Leche merengada
A milk ice cream with cinnamon and lemon, very Valencian.

Coffee

Bluebell for own-roasted specialty coffee and weekend brunch; La Ola Fresca for a neighbourhood breakfast in Benimaclet. Neither is an after-dinner stop: they are morning or lunchtime plans.

Bluebell Coffee

café, Ruzafa

Documentary research

Own-roasted specialty coffee in Ruzafa, in the morning or at weekend brunch.

The catch: Coffee after dinner: its website says it closes at 16:00.

La Ola Fresca

deli-café, Benimaclet

Documentary research

A daytime neighbourhood plan: breakfast, brunch or a simple lunch in a cosy room; reviews highlight the service and the coffee.

The catch: Rounding off a late dinner.
