Esmorzar: Valencia’s substantial mid-morning ritual

If you searched for ‘Valencian brunch’, start here. Esmorzar is its own tradition: usually a substantial savoury break in the morning, often built around a bocadillo. It is not simply eggs Benedict translated into Valencian. Ask what the deal includes: sandwich size, peanuts and olives, drink, coffee and any supplements.

Where to start in the city

Cassalla and Cremaet are contemporary concepts from the same group; they do not share prices and neither is on the seafront.

Bar Cassalla

esmorzar bar, Abastos / Arrancapins

Documentary research

A first taste of esmorzar opposite the Mercado de Abastos, with a charcoal grill in full view.

The catch: Booking: that service is walk-in. Nor a beach plan: it is in Abastos.

€8.50 per person, partial total

Bar Cremaet

esmorzar bar, Avenida del Puerto

Documentary research

Esmorzar on a terrace facing the Casa del Médico, finished with the house cremaet.

The catch: A beach plan: it is at the start of Avenida del Puerto, not on the seafront.

€6 per person, base price

La Pascuala

esmorzar bodega, Malvarrosa / seafront promenade

The old-school bodega esmorzar, before a stroll round Malvarrosa or Cabanyal.

The catch: Sundays: closed according to both website and menu. Nor for following it with a huge paella out of habit.

€14 per person, partial total

Glossary

Esmorzar
Valencia’s mid-morning meal; what is included varies by venue.
Bocadillo / medio
A whole or half sandwich; ask whether a half is available.
Gasto / picaeta
Nibbles served before the sandwich, such as peanuts and olives; varies by venue.
Cremaet
Coffee prepared with alcohol in the traditional way; don’t assume flaming removes all of it. Order ordinary coffee if you prefer.
Cassalla
An anise spirit, not a local word for water.

Alcohol-free, no pressure

Ordinary coffee or an alcohol-free drink, with no pressure on children, drivers or anyone who doesn’t drink. Neither cremaet nor cassalla is compulsory for cultural admission.

What about Fernandet?

It is in Sueca, outside Valencia city, and today opens at 13:00 Friday to Sunday: a rice plan, not an esmorzar one. It is here for its esmorzar history, not to get you up early.

Fernandet

restaurant (day trip), Sueca beach (day trip)

Documentary researchDay trip

A rice day trip: reviews highlight the rice dishes, attentive service and a relaxed, family feel.

The catch: Breakfast: today it opens at lunchtime, not for esmorzar. Nor is it a walk from the Marina: you need a car or transport.
