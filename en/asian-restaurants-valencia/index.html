Asian food in Valencia: it isn’t all sushi buffets

“Asian” is a word that covers too much. An omakase at the counter, a buffet with a tablet and a tabletop grill have nothing in common except the chopsticks. Choose the plan first, then the place.

À la carte Japanese, for a treat

Nozomi for counter omakase in Ruzafa; Komori, in the Westin, for nigiri with a Valencian twist. Both need a booking and a healthy budget.

Nozomi Sushi Bar

restaurant, Ruzafa

Documentary research

A Japanese counter in Ruzafa for a treat: omakase and a booking made well ahead.

The catch: Two people with €40 for everything, or a spur-of-the-moment dinner.

€70 per person, partial total

Komori

restaurant, Mestalla / Alameda

Documentary research

High-end Japanese in the Westin, with Valencian touches in the nigiri: one for an occasion.

The catch: A quick or cheap meal: it is a hotel Japanese restaurant with Repsol Suns in its history, and you pay for it.

Japanese buffet, for when you’re hungry

Aoyama at lunchtime if you’re after fish; Kaisen if you like sauced rolls; Kyoto for groups and families. Compulsory drinks extra and a table time limit: not a place to linger.

Aoyama

sushi buffet, Eixample / Colón / Cánovas

Documentary researchA bargain, with conditions

A lunchtime Japanese buffet for those who come for the fish rather than the sauces; dessert is included.

The catch: Light eaters: compare with an à la carte Japanese restaurant first.

€18 per person, partial total

Kaisen Centro

sushi buffet, Xàtiva / station area

Documentary research

Arriving hungry and into sauced fusion rolls, right by Estació del Nord.

The catch: Anyone avoiding cream cheese and sauces.

€20.50 per person, partial total

Kyoto Valladolid

sushi buffet, Benimaclet

Documentary research

Hungry group or family meals that want variety: reviews highlight fresh food, lots of choice and no waiting when you reorder.

The catch: A quiet dinner for two: it is a big buffet built for groups and get-togethers.

Korean, with a grill

Éter by the portion in the city; Hankook as a buffet, outside Valencia city.

Éter Artes y Ciencias

à la carte Korean barbecue, City of Arts and Sciences

Documentary researchSeveral branches

Trying Korean barbecue without the buffet blow-out: order the portions you want and grill them at the table.

The catch: An all-you-can-eat dinner: it is à la carte. Nor a serious conversation while minding the pork belly.

€16 per person, partial total

Hankook Alfafar

Korean barbecue buffet, Alfafar (outside Valencia city)

Documentary researchOutside Valencia city

Hungry groups who enjoy grilling in rounds: tabletop grill plus sushi, at a buffet price.

The catch: Light eaters, or anyone wanting one specific dish: unlimited access adds little.

€18.90 per person, base price

Fusion and a show

Canalla for globe-trotting sharing plates; Voltereta Bali to eat inside a stage set, best at the weekday lunch price.
