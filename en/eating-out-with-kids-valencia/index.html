Eating out with a baby or an active child, without screens

A children’s menu is not enough for a child who needs to move. We tell you separately what each venue advertises and what an outside source reports.

Five tips before you choose

  • With a restless child, look for a play space: a children’s menu is not enough.
  • A terrace is not an enclosed area: plan on keeping an eye on them yourselves.
  • A park nearby helps burn off energy afterwards, but supervision is still yours.
  • If you want zero screens, ask three things: is there screen-free play, does the venue have that policy, and is there a TV in the room.
  • When you book, ask for a table with a view of the play area.
