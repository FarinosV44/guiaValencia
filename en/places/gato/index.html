Gato Blanco

family restaurant, Zaidía / Trinitat / Viveros

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Families who want to eat while the children play in the same place, with a gluten-free kitchen.

Not your plan if…

Mondays and Tuesdays: closed.

What to order

The published menu of the day; check drinks and days.

What might disappoint

Whether you can see the play area from your table depends on where you sit: ask for it when you book.

Before you go

The venue declares a gluten-free kitchen: confirm your needs and allergens. Ask for a table overlooking the play area.

What’s on offer

Self-declared gluten-free kitchen, children's area and board games.
