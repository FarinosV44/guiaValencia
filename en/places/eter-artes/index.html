Éter Artes y Ciencias

à la carte Korean barbecue, City of Arts and Sciences

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Trying Korean barbecue without the buffet blow-out: order the portions you want and grill them at the table.

Not your plan if…

An all-you-can-eat dinner: it is à la carte. Nor a serious conversation while minding the pork belly.

What to order

Bulgogi or pork belly by the portion, plus the side dishes: start with a few portions and order more as you go.

What might disappoint

As it is by the portion, a big appetite pushes the bill up fast: if you want to eat without counting, the Korean buffet is a different plan.

Before you go

Éter has more than one venue: this is the Arts and Sciences one; check the address on their website before setting off. Drinks extra.

What’s on offer

Korean barbecue adaptation by the portion, with side dishes.
