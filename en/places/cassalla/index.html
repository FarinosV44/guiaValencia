Bar Cassalla

esmorzar bar, Abastos / Arrancapins

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

A first taste of esmorzar opposite the Mercado de Abastos, with a charcoal grill in full view.

Not your plan if…

Booking: that service is walk-in. Nor a beach plan: it is in Abastos.

What to order

Build your sandwich from the grill (longaniza, morcilla, chistorra or pork belly) with patatas a lo pobre or fried egg; the pork-belly Americano is the house one. The full esmorzar comes with peanuts, olives, a drink and coffee.

What might disappoint

It is not a century-old tavern: it is a new concept from the Gastroadictos group, and big (about 250 seats). For the old-school bar, La Pascuala.

Before you go

Weekends and holidays +€1.50. Do not copy its prices to Cremaet, even though they share a group.
