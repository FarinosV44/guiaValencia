La Ola Fresca

deli-café, Benimaclet

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

A daytime neighbourhood plan: breakfast, brunch or a simple lunch in a cosy room; reviews highlight the service and the coffee.

Not your plan if…

Rounding off a late dinner.

What to order

Breakfast or brunch with eggs, good coffee or tea, and a slice of cake.

What might disappoint

The children's books mentioned by a local association are not a play centre.

Before you go

It fills up: book. There is a terrace and a family feel.

What reviews say

Highly rated breakfasts in a cosy room; book.
