Véneta Ribera

ice-cream shop, Central Market / Sant Francesc

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Ice cream with somewhere to sit after lunch around Sant Francesc or at Linaje.

Not your plan if…

Confusing it with the Brodadors branch.

What to order

The Véneta classics: grandma’s biscuit, pistachio or Sicilian orange.

What might disappoint

A bit pricey for a scoop, like the whole chain according to reviews.

Before you go

There is seating upstairs: better than Brodadors if you want to stop for a while.

What reviews say

The Véneta with seating upstairs; pistachio and hazelnut are the favourites.

  • For · Food Pistachio and hazelnut stand out; good texture, not too sweet.
  • For · Atmosphere A cosy seating area upstairs.
  • Against · Price Some find it expensive.
  • Against · Service Some comments about curt staff.
