Habitual

restaurant, Eixample / Colón / Cánovas

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

A compact plan: lunch and horchata in the same Mercado de Colón.

Not your plan if…

Sundays: the official website says closed, although Michelin shows Sunday hours.

What to order

The long menu brings together well-known Camarena dishes; the Michelin Guide mentions the Galician beef tartare with egg-yolk foam, the organic tomato tatin and fried eggs with lobster and sobrasada.

What might disappoint

There is no children’s menu (they adapt dishes), and Fridays and Saturdays fill up: book.

Before you go

It is on the lower floor of the Mercado de Colón; with a pushchair, ask about the lift. Weekday lunchtimes are quieter.

What reviews say

An affordable way to try well-known Camarena dishes, with vegetables in the lead.

  • For · Food A long, affordable menu with well-known Camarena dishes; vegetables lead.

Read on Guía Michelin, October 2026. We summarise what recurs or helps you order; we don’t copy reviews.

What’s on offer

Mediterranean cooking focused on produce and vegetables.

Dessert here or out?

For something sweet, head to La Romana Joaquín Costa or Daniel Mercado de Colón, in the same area.

Check the sweet stop’s hours for the time you actually leave; the ones that close early won’t work after dinner.
