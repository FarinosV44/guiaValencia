Véneta Brodadors

ice-cream shop, El Carmen / La Seu

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

A compact sweet stop on the way to the Cathedral and Plaza de la Virgen.

Not your plan if…

Anyone who hates queues: there are some at peak times.

What to order

Galleta de la abuela (grandma’s biscuit), the flavour that made its name; Pistacchio Paradise or Sicilian orange if you want something less sweet.

What might disappoint

Reviews find it a bit pricey and the service sometimes curt. The awards (best gelato in Spain and Europe at the Gelato Festival) don’t guarantee you’ll like every flavour.

Before you go

Ask to taste before choosing. If there is a queue, Véneta Ribera has seating upstairs.

What reviews say

Award-winning gelato with good texture; watch out for weekend surcharges on some flavours.
