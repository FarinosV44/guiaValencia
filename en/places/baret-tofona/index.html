El Baret de la Tòfona

bar-restaurant, Castellar-Oliveral

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Rice and market cooking in Castellar, worth a deliberate trip with time to spare.

Not your plan if…

A cheap neighbourhood set menu: a recent review found no set menu and recalls around €40–50 per person.

What to order

The rice of the day: ask the price, the minimum number of portions and whether it must be ordered ahead. One review recommends starters, rice and apple tart.

What might disappoint

Viviana (5★, four months ago) says the format has changed, there was no set menu and parking was hard. Allow time and don’t count on parking at the door.

Before you go

This is not La Tòfona in Cánovas (Comte d'Altea 9): do not apply its menu or reviews here.

What reviews say

Generous portions and market cooking; ask the price and allow time to park.
