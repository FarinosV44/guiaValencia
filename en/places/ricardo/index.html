Bar Ricardo

bar, La Petxina / Extramurs

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Classic neighbourhood tapas with serious produce in La Petxina: bravas, ensaladilla and cuttlefish.

Not your plan if…

Ordering seafood by weight without asking: the bill moves into another league.

What to order

Patatas bravas, Russian salad (ensaladilla) and cuttlefish with mayonnaise.

What might disappoint

A 5★ review describes €250 for two with seafood and found it worth it: one occasion, not the typical bill. Another got a table without booking for about an hour: not a rule.

Before you go

Ask the weight and estimated price before confirming seafood. Calling itself a 'bar' does not cap the bill.

What reviews say

Quality produce and efficient service; seafood takes the bill into another league.
