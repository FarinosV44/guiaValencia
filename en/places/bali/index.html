Voltereta Bali

Who it suits

Eating inside a Balinese stage set: it makes sense at the weekday lunch price.

Not your plan if…

A long, lingering lunch: one review mentions a 90-minute table limit.

Our labels

  • You pay for the set The décor is what everyone talks about, and the mains convince less than the starters and sushi. (google.com, review due by 24 December 2026)
  • Best on the set menu The show makes sense at the weekday lunch menu price. (volteretarestaurante.com, review due by 24 November 2026)

What to order

The weekday lunch menu; one review rates starters and sushi above the mains.

What might disappoint

According to reviews, the mains convince less than the starters and sushi. Calling it “all show” would be unfair, but you are paying for the set.

Before you go

When booking, ask about the table time limit: that tells you whether there is room to linger.

What reviews say

People come for the set and the lunch menu; starters and sushi beat the mains.
