Antigua Lechería

ice-cream shop, Ruzafa

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

A very Ruzafa sweet stop, with homemade ice cream in inventive flavours; Repsol recommends it.

Not your plan if…

Anyone who wants the usual ice cream: people come here for the inventive flavours.

What to order

Leche merengada, the house flavour; and if they have them, apple with cinnamon and walnuts or mascarpone with pistachio.

What might disappoint

Flavours rotate: the one you were told about may not be on that day. Ask and taste.

Before you go

Closed on Mondays. Step-free entrance, good for pushchairs; takeaway too.

What reviews say

House-made ice cream with lots of vegan flavours and staff who know their stuff; take it away and eat it in the square.

  • For · Food Hazelnut, lemon pie, dark chocolate and lemon-basil are among the most mentioned; lots of vegan flavours.
  • For · Service Owners and staff friendly and expert, vegan options included.
  • For · Price Affordable for what it is.
  • Against · Food Fewer vegan flavours in winter.
  • Against · Atmosphere Little seating: better as takeaway.

What’s on offer

Artisan ice cream in inventive flavours; leche merengada, apple with cinnamon and walnuts, mascarpone with pistachio.
