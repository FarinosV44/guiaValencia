La Pepica

rice restaurant, Malvarrosa / seafront promenade

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Rice, a stroll and ice cream along the same Neptuno promenade.

Not your plan if…

Anyone in a hurry: the venue itself warns of at least 40–45 minutes for rice, longer at peak times.

What to order

Rice (minimum two portions). Opinions on the paella valenciana and the vegetable rice are mixed: if in doubt, ask which is best that day.

What might disappoint

In some 3★ reviews the setting and service come off better than the food. Come for rice by the sea, without expecting the rice of your life.

Before you go

With a restless child, factor in the wait for the rice.

What reviews say

Rice with a view and good service; the cooking doesn’t always match the setting.

  • For · Atmosphere Setting and location praised even by diners disappointed by the food.
  • For · Service Service well rated.
  • Against · Food Mixed experiences with the paella valenciana and the vegetable rice in 3★ reviews.

What’s on offer

Rice dishes and a terrace on the seafront promenade.

Dessert here or out?

For something sweet, head to Véneta Neptuno, in the same area.

Check the sweet stop’s hours for the time you actually leave; the ones that close early won’t work after dinner.
