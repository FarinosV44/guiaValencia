Pizzería Km0

pizzeria, Benimaclet

Documentary researchGood value

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Neapolitan pizza on 48-hour dough with local produce, and the best vegan section on our list, in Benimaclet.

Not your plan if…

Anyone who only wants the usual Margherita: the seasonal pizzas are the point here.

Our labels

  • Good value Pizzas from €10.50 on the menu, and reviews highlight local produce and fair prices. (wanderlog.com, review due by 24 December 2026)

What to order

A Margherita to judge the dough, then a seasonal one or one of the pistachio pizzas, the ones reviews mention most.

What might disappoint

Few tables: without a booking it is easy to miss out.

Before you go

Book. Dinner every day, lunch only at weekends; takeaway too. The menu changes with the season.

What reviews say

Local produce, well-risen dough and the best vegan choice on our list.

  • For · Food Well-risen dough and inventive toppings such as pistachio.
  • For · Food Plenty of vegan choice.
  • For · Service Friendly staff.
  • For · Price Fair prices.

Read on Wanderlog, October 2026. We summarise what recurs or helps you order; we don’t copy reviews.

What’s on offer

Italian dough with local, seasonal ingredients; a large vegan section.

Dessert here or out?

For something sweet, head to Els Sariers, in the same area.

Check the sweet stop’s hours for the time you actually leave; the ones that close early won’t work after dinner.
