Hippolatte

café with play area, Ruzafa

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

A 3–5-year-old who needs to move; the value is the play, not the coffee.

Not your plan if…

Anyone after calm, or with a very young baby: it is designed for 3-to-5-year-olds who need to move.

What to order

A coffee and a snack while they play: people come here for the play.

What might disappoint

One review complains about price and service. The plan is the play: coffee comes second.

Before you go

Ask about sessions, ages, capacity and price when you book; hours change in summer.

What reviews say
