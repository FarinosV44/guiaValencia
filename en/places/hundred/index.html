Hundred Burgers

burger restaurant, Central Market / Sant Francesc

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Anyone who wants to try the burger Burgerdudes named the world’s best: 100 seats and plenty of demand.

Not your plan if…

Anyone after a quiet, hidden burger joint: this is the most famous one in town.

What to order

The Satisfaction (crispy bacon, smoked mayo) or the Loser (caramelised onion, gochujang). Dry-aged Galician beef and buns baked in-house.

What might disappoint

An award reflects that jury’s taste, not yours. TripAdvisor has one-off complaints (dry nuggets, curt service, no vegan desserts) and one blogger finds it overrated.

Before you go

Book on their website. It is at Sant Vicent Màrtir 44, with step-free access. Burgerdudes is not Michelin, but for burgers it is the benchmark.

What reviews say

Generous burgers and staff who help you choose; complaints are one-offs.

  • For · Food Tasty, generous burgers.
  • For · Service Staff help you choose, and reviews keep saying so.
  • For · Price Good value.
  • Against · Food One-off complaints: dry nuggets, no vegan dessert.

Read on Gastroranking and TripAdvisor, October 2026. We summarise what recurs or helps you order; we don’t copy reviews.

What’s on offer

Dry-aged beef burgers on house-baked buns, awarded by Burgerdudes.

Dessert here or out?

For something sweet, head to La Romana San Vicente or Véneta Ribera, in the same area.

Check the sweet stop’s hours for the time you actually leave; the ones that close early won’t work after dinner.
