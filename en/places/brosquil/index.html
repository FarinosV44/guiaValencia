Alquería del Brosquil

rice restaurant in a farmhouse, Castellar-Oliveral

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Rice in a farmhouse among market gardens: a long lunch worth the trip, not a stop on a stroll.

Not your plan if…

More than two different rice dishes per table: the menu limits it to two.

What to order

Dry or creamy rice; for dessert, orange tiramisu or mille-feuille.

What might disappoint

Two recent comments on aggregators mention too much salt and an overcooked rice for fifteen people. With a big group, ask how they split the paellas.

Before you go

Book by phone. The farmhouse and courtyard are not a supervised children’s area: with kids, take turns.

What reviews say

Well-cooked rice; with big groups, ask for it split across several paellas.

  • For · Food Well-cooked rice, even if one diner prefers another style of paella.
  • Against · Food One diner found the rice salty on two visits.
  • Against · Food Another, an overcooked rice for a group of fifteen.
