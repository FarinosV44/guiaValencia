La Pascuala

esmorzar bodega, Malvarrosa / seafront promenade

Documentary researchGood value

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

The old-school bodega esmorzar, before a stroll round Malvarrosa or Cabanyal.

Not your plan if…

Sundays: closed according to both website and menu. Nor for following it with a huge paella out of habit.

Our labels

  • Good value A full esmorzar with coffee for €14 on weekdays, and reviews agree the portion is huge for the price. (gastroranking.es, review due by 24 December 2026)

What to order

Full esmorzar with coffee (€14) or half (€9.50), Monday to Friday.

What might disappoint

Its fame comes from years of esmorzar awards: context, not a guarantee for every sandwich today. And there is no esmorzar menu on Saturdays.

Before you go

The esmorzar menu runs Monday to Friday; it is closed on Sunday and shuts mid-afternoon on Saturday.

What reviews say

Huge sandwiches at a fair price in an old-school bodega; go early and be patient.

  • For · Food Huge, tasty sandwiches: a half is plenty; the tortilla and the cremaet are praised.
  • For · Price Modest prices for the portion.
  • Against · Booking and waits It fills up fast and waits are long.
  • Against · Service Slow service at peak times and some curt staff.
  • Against · Food Some find too much bread or feel standards have slipped.

Read on Gastroranking, October 2026. We summarise what recurs or helps you order; we don’t copy reviews.

What’s on offer

An old bodega with an esmorzar tradition near the beach.

Dessert here or out?

For something sweet, head to Véneta Neptuno, in the same area.

Check the sweet stop’s hours for the time you actually leave; the ones that close early won’t work after dinner.
