La Sastrería

restaurant, Cabanyal / Canyamelar

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Seafood over embers in one of the most strikingly designed rooms in Cabanyal.

Not your plan if…

Anyone after the lowest price: the published menu is €44.

What to order

Lightly seared red prawn, razor clams and the catch of the day over embers; or the Menú Bordado if you want a tour of the kitchen.

What might disappoint

The bar and the restaurant are two separate spaces, and the restaurant entrance is round the corner: say which you want when you book.

Before you go

The €30 price in old guides no longer applies: the published menu is €44.

What reviews say

The food convinces more than the room: local produce handled very well in a space that feels cool rather than cosy.

  • For · Food The most praised: prawns, croquettes, anchovies and tasty dishes built on local produce.
  • For · Service Bar staff highly rated; they found a table for a group.
  • Against · Atmosphere Some find the room cold or austere.
  • Against · Service Some comments about correct but not warm service, and chipped crockery.

Read on Gastroranking, October 2026. We summarise what recurs or helps you order; we don’t copy reviews.

What’s on offer

Seafood cooking in a strongly designed space.

Dessert here or out?

For something sweet, head to Gelática Ice Concept, in the same area.
