Kuzina

restaurant, El Carmen / La Seu

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Mezze and moussaka in El Carmen, for when you want something different without ending up at an Asian buffet.

Not your plan if…

Turning up on a whim: the room is small and fills up; book.

What to order

Mezze to start (spanakopita, dolmades, taramosalata), moussaka (there is a vegetarian one) and baklava, distinctive enough to stay for dessert.

What might disappoint

Some find it pricey for what it is (set menus at €35 and €48 without drinks), and the wait for mains drags.

Before you go

Book; at lunchtime the terrace gets the sun. It is run by Alkis and Lydia, who used to be on Calle Salvador: some listings still give the old address.

What reviews say

Tasty food and good advice; it costs, and the mains take patience.
