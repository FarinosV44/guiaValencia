Canalla Bistro

restaurant, Ruzafa

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Globe-trotting sharing plates in Ruzafa, for couples or friends who want to try a bit of everything.

Not your plan if…

Anyone planning another dessert stop: the menu already includes desserts.

What to order

If ordering à la carte, vary the techniques rather than stacking tempura, gyoza and fried dishes.

What might disappoint

One critical review (2★) complains of too much frying and salt, and unexplained changes; the same diner praised the desserts. Order across techniques and save room for the end.

Before you go

The homepage sells vouchers at other prices: they are not the same offer as the menu.

What reviews say

Good for exploring flavours if you order across techniques; the desserts come out well even in the critical review.

  • For · Food Two recent diners praise the fusion cooking.
  • For · Service Service well rated.
  • Against · Food One critical review (2★) complains of too much frying and salt, and unexplained changes.
  • For · Atmosphere One review describes it as quiet.

What’s on offer

Globe-trotting fusion cooking and sharing menus.

Dessert here or out?

Stay for dessert: it’s already included, so the extra cost is zero. Going out for another sweet is a separate treat, not part of the plan.
