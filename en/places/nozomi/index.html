Nozomi Sushi Bar

restaurant, Ruzafa

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

A Japanese counter in Ruzafa for a treat: omakase and a booking made well ahead.

Not your plan if…

Two people with €40 for everything, or a spur-of-the-moment dinner.

What to order

The omakase: it is what Repsol highlights and the best way to watch the counter at work.

What might disappoint

A 1★ review (4 months ago) comes from someone who could not get a table: a warning not to improvise, not a verdict on the sushi.

Before you go

Book well ahead: one review comes from someone who turned up without a booking and didn’t get in. There is no play area.

What reviews say

The sushi and service convince; what fails is turning up without a booking.

  • For · Food The sushi is highly rated.
  • For · Service Service praised.
  • Against · Booking and waits One diner got no table without a booking.

Read on Google, October 2026. We summarise what recurs or helps you order; we don’t copy reviews.

What’s on offer

Japanese cooking, sushi and counter seating.

Dessert here or out?

For something sweet, head to Antigua Lechería, in the same area.

Check the sweet stop’s hours for the time you actually leave; the ones that close early won’t work after dinner.
