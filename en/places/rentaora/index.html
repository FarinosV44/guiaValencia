La Rentaora

tavern, El Carmen / La Seu

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Snacks and wine on Mossén Sorell: cheese, charcuterie, then on with your stroll through El Carmen.

Not your plan if…

A full dinner when you are properly hungry: this is grazing.

What to order

Goat’s cheese with chutney and walnuts, baked brie with jam, cured sausage and, to finish, the warm brownie. With wine: the list is one people praise.

What might disappoint

These are snacking portions: with a big appetite the bill grows fast (one review mentions about €36 for five tapas and three wines).

Before you go

It fills up: book at busy times. There are gluten-free options.

What reviews say

Cosy snacking with a good wine list and reasonable prices.
