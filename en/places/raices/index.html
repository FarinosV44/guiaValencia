Raíces

pizzeria, El Carmen / La Seu

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Contemporary pizza from a short menu by the Cathedral, for groups of two to four who don’t mind waiting for a table.

Not your plan if…

Needing a guaranteed seating time: no bookings.

What to order

A Margherita to judge dough and bake; reviews describe an airy crust and balanced toppings.

What might disappoint

Reviews talk about the dough, not the desserts: come for the pizza, not for a sweet finish.

Before you go

No bookings and groups of up to four: arrive early or accept the wait.

What reviews say

Serious contemporary pizza: reviews talk about the dough in detail.

  • For · Food An airy crust and well-balanced toppings.

Read on Google, October 2026. We summarise what recurs or helps you order; we don’t copy reviews.

What’s on offer

A pizza-focused menu near the Cathedral.

Dessert here or out?

Pick one, not both: Tiramisu here (€7) or head to Véneta Brodadors.

Check the sweet stop’s hours for the time you actually leave; the ones that close early won’t work after dinner.
