Gelática Ice Concept

ice-cream shop and patisserie, Cabanyal / Canyamelar

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Ice cream and brownies in Cabanyal, for the afternoon or after lunch.

Not your plan if…

After dinner: it closes early and the last half hour is takeaway only.

What to order

Reviews mention chocolate with turrón, lemon sorbet, pistachio and brownie. A single scoop is not the only reason to go.

What might disappoint

Flavours rotate: the one you were told about may not be on that day.

Before you go

It closes early and the last half hour is takeaway only: come in the afternoon.

What reviews say

Don’t come just for a scoop: the brownie and pastries justify the stop too.
