Petit Niu

café-restaurant with play area, Mestalla / Alameda

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Families with children aged 0–6 who need screen-free play, as the main lunch-and-play plan.

Not your plan if…

After dinner: it closes early. Nor for leaving the child alone: an adult must stay with them.

What to order

Simple food in service of the play: come to play and eat something, not the other way round.

What might disappoint

Its own pages disagree on passes and supplements: ask for the price when you book.

Before you go

Call ahead to find out what entry includes, how many adults can come in and what extra time costs.

What reviews say

A very good start in reviews: space, staff and food.
