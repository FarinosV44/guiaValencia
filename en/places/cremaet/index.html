Bar Cremaet

esmorzar bar, Avenida del Puerto

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Esmorzar on a terrace facing the Casa del Médico, finished with the house cremaet.

Not your plan if…

A beach plan: it is at the start of Avenida del Puerto, not on the seafront.

What to order

The Cabanyalera (titaina and fried egg) or the Almussafes (sobrasada, cheese, bacon, onion and egg). And the cremaet, of course: coffee with flamed rum, lemon peel and cinnamon.

What might disappoint

The sandwich price does not include a drink or coffee: it is not a fixed full esmorzar.

Before you go

Same group as Cassalla, with its own prices. A terrace facing the Casa del Médico and sandwiches all day.

What reviews say

An old-style bar in a new format, with sandwiches all day.
