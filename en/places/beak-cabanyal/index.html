Beak & Trotter

burger restaurant, Cabanyal / Canyamelar

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Hungry friends who want to share ribs and smoky burgers; reviews highlight generous portions and staff who give good recommendations.

Not your plan if…

An intimate dinner: a small room that is nearly always full.

What to order

Beef short rib and the Big Smoke or the double cheeseburger. Reviews insist: order the fries.

What might disappoint

Portions are big: under-order and add more. Without a booking, it is easy to miss out.

Before you go

Their website puts them at La Reina 148 (Cabanyal); some listings still show the old Conde de Altea premises, now closed. Book.

What reviews say

Smoke, big portions and good advice from staff; book.
