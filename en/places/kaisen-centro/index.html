Kaisen Centro

sushi buffet, Xàtiva / station area

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Arriving hungry and into sauced fusion rolls, right by Estació del Nord.

Not your plan if…

Anyone avoiding cream cheese and sauces.

What to order

Check what the evening or weekend rate adds before paying it; if sauced sushi is your thing, there is plenty to choose from.

What might disappoint

A 3★ review credits the value but criticises the rush at the start and the cream cheese in many pieces: if that isn’t your thing, ask for pieces without it.

Before you go

Compulsory drink extra. Older PDFs show other prices; its 'number one' claim is self-promotion, not our ranking.

What reviews say

Good value if you enjoy fusion; if you avoid cream cheese, say so.
