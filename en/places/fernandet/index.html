Fernandet

restaurant (day trip), Sueca beach (day trip)

Documentary researchDay trip

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

A rice day trip: reviews highlight the rice dishes, attentive service and a relaxed, family feel.

Not your plan if…

Breakfast: today it opens at lunchtime, not for esmorzar. Nor is it a walk from the Marina: you need a car or transport.

What to order

Paella valenciana or another rice; to start, octopus with prawns or homemade ham croquettes.

What might disappoint

The odd complaint is about starters (small squid portion, ordinary bravas), not the rice: come for the rice.

Before you go

Book lunch by phone (633 97 90 57). The website gives Daniel Martí 5 and an older listing 16D, with the same phone number: ask about the entrance when you book.

What reviews say

Come for the rice: it is what gets the most praise.
