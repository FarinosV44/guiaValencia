Anyora

bodega restaurant, Cabanyal / Canyamelar

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Valencian food chosen for culinary reasons, not just the décor.

Not your plan if…

Sundays: the kitchen is closed according to its website.

What to order

To share: tripe (callos), anchovies (boquerones) and cuttlefish (sepia). For dessert, the cremaet ice cream has Valencian character. Reviews mention aubergine with cheese.

What might disappoint

The visible reviews are all positive, but too few to speak of consistency. Come for the stews, not the décor.

Before you go

The cremaet ice cream is suggested from the menu, not because reviews validated it; check alcohol and allergens.

Dessert here or out?
