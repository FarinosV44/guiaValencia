Aoyama

sushi buffet, Eixample / Colón / Cánovas

Documentary researchA bargain, with conditions

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

A lunchtime Japanese buffet for those who come for the fish rather than the sauces; dessert is included.

Not your plan if…

Light eaters: compare with an à la carte Japanese restaurant first.

Our labels

  • A bargain, with conditions Weekday lunch buffet at €18 with dessert included, against Kaisen’s €20.50 base price. Conditions: compulsory drinks extra and €2 for each plate left. (aoyama.es, review due by 24 November 2026)

What to order

A small first round: one plain piece, one with sauce and one hot dish. A review mentions fish that does not need much sauce.

What might disappoint

One review complains that QR ordering is barely explained and that, at peak times, the table gets cleared with dishes still to come. Order early, in short rounds.

Before you go

Minimum drink extra, coffee extra, €2 per uneaten dish. Dessert is included, so no need to go out for another.

What reviews say

Good food for a buffet; QR ordering can confuse at peak times.

  • For · Food Fish that doesn’t need much sauce.
  • Against · Service One review says the QR system is barely explained and some dishes arrived late.

What’s on offer

Japanese buffet with clearly stated lunchtime conditions.

Dessert here or out?

Stay for dessert: it’s already included, so the extra cost is zero. Going out for another sweet is a separate treat, not part of the plan.

Children and families

Cost: Child 3–5: €8; 6–10: €12 (day-menu PDF).
