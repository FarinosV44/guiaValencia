Soul Coffee Beer

burger restaurant

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Burger-list chasers: Soul has made Burgerdudes’ world top 10.

Not your plan if…

A coffee stop because of the name: it is a burger restaurant.

What to order

The Notorius, the one everyone mentions, and save room: reviews rave about the desserts.

What might disappoint

Plain décor, and one review got a lukewarm burger. The menu weight is raw weight.

Before you go

The flagship is in Paiporta (C/ San Pascual 12), just outside the city: go on purpose and book at weekends.

What reviews say

Burger and desserts highly praised at its Paiporta venue.
