Véneta Neptuno

ice-cream shop, Malvarrosa / seafront promenade

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Ice cream on the seafront promenade, the same one as La Pepica.

Not your plan if…

Confusing it with the Ribera branch.

What to order

The Véneta classics: grandma’s biscuit, pistachio or Sicilian orange.

What might disappoint

A bit pricey for a scoop, like the whole chain according to reviews.

Before you go

Out of season the hours change: if you are going late, call.

What’s on offer

Gelato on the seafront promenade.
