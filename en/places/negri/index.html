La Negri

bar-restaurant, Benimaclet

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Globe-trotting neighbourhood cooking in Benimaclet, with a cheesecake that has a reputation, and a terrace.

Not your plan if…

Mondays and Tuesdays: closed according to its website.

What to order

The weekday lunch menu; and the cheesecake, the house’s best-known dish.

What might disappoint

It fills up: without a booking it is a lottery. Closed Monday and Tuesday.

Before you go

Book. Terrace and high chairs. Check the menu price on their website: guide prices age fast.

What’s on offer

A well-travelled menu, tapas and mixed roots.

Dessert here or out?

For something sweet, head to Els Sariers, in the same area.

Check the sweet stop’s hours for the time you actually leave; the ones that close early won’t work after dinner.
