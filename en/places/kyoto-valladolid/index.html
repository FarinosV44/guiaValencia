Kyoto Valladolid

sushi buffet, Benimaclet

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Hungry group or family meals that want variety: reviews highlight fresh food, lots of choice and no waiting when you reorder.

Not your plan if…

A quiet dinner for two: it is a big buffet built for groups and get-togethers.

What to order

Pick a tier (A, B or C) by checking which specialities each includes, and start with the sushi and salmon, the dishes reviews mention most.

What might disappoint

The tatami rooms work out expensive for a family, and one review mentions a €3-per-person booking supplement.

Before you go

Book, and ask about the supplement and the tatami rooms when you do. The top tier’s specialities are not in the basic one.

What reviews say

A family buffet with fresh food and no waiting for refills.

  • For · Food Fresh food and plenty of variety.
  • For · Service Quick and friendly: no waiting when you reorder.
  • Against · Price Tatami rooms pricey for families, and a booking supplement.
