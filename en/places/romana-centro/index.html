La Romana San Vicente

ice-cream shop, Central Market / Sant Francesc

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Ice cream right on Sant Vicent, with pistachio and Italian creams you can taste first.

Not your plan if…

Anyone expecting the chain's full range: choose from what is in the cabinet.

What to order

Pistachio, black coffee or lemon, and look out for the flavour of the month. They let you taste: ask.

What might disappoint

Long queues and the odd complaint that scoops have shrunk. Being a chain makes it neither worse nor better.

Before you go

Go outside peak times. No artificial colours, and several vegan options.

What reviews say
