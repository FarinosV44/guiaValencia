Central Bar

bar, Central Market / Sant Francesc

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Ricard Camarena’s sandwich at the Mercado Central counter: a market esmorzar or tapas at lunchtime.

Not your plan if…

Dinner: the kitchen closes at 15:00 (15:30 on Friday and Saturday).

What to order

Two different plans: the Camarena sandwich for esmorzar, or bravas, salt-cod fritters and ensaladilla for a tapas lunch. They don’t cost the same.

What might disappoint

Reviews find it pricey for a market bar, the service correct but cool, and there is no traditional esmorzar: it is Camarena’s sandwich, not the neighbourhood breakfast.

Before you go

Closed on Sundays and public holidays. Arrive before 11:00 to get a seat at the bar; on Saturdays, better to book.

What reviews say

Top-quality produce for a market sandwich; you pay for it, and the service is correct, no more.

What’s on offer

Sandwiches and tapas inside the Central Market.

Dessert here or out?

For something sweet, head to La Romana San Vicente or Véneta Ribera, in the same area.

Check the sweet stop’s hours for the time you actually leave; the ones that close early won’t work after dinner.
