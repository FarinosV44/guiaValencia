Casa Carmela

rice restaurant, Malvarrosa / seafront promenade

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Wood-fired rice in Malvarrosa, for a rice lunch where you don’t hold back.

Not your plan if…

A cheap meal, or an impromptu paella valenciana: it needs ordering 24 hours ahead.

What to order

Wood-fired rice: it is what justifies the visit, according to reviews. Go easy on the starters.

What might disappoint

One review found the dessert ordinary. Another objects to the QR-code menu.

Before you go

If you want a screen-free meal, ask for a printed menu when you arrive.

What reviews say

People come for the rice; book ahead and you can skip dessert.
