RiFF

fine dining, Eixample / Colón / Cánovas

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

A special occasion built on local produce; ask for the counter if you like watching the kitchen work.

Not your plan if…

Anyone who needs natural light or a specific table: ask for it when you book.

What to order

Pick a format: the lunch menu to get to know the place, EXCEL·LENT for an occasion. Ask what each includes when you book.

What might disappoint

One TheFork review missed explanations of the menu and natural light; recent ones praise the service. If you want the story behind each dish, ask.

Before you go

The EXCEL·LENT (€158.80, wine pairing €85 extra) is on their website; the lunch menu (€89.80 on TheFork) is the way in. Tuesday to Saturday.

What reviews say

Menu and service highly rated in recent reviews.

What’s on offer

Valencian, seasonal produce in a tasting menu.

Dessert here or out?

For something sweet, head to La Romana Joaquín Costa or Daniel Mercado de Colón, in the same area.

Check the sweet stop’s hours for the time you actually leave; the ones that close early won’t work after dinner.
