Filippa’s

pizzeria, Ruzafa

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

A table of mixed tastes in Ruzafa: Neapolitan pizza for some, pasta for others.

Not your plan if…

Strict coeliacs: having gluten-free options is not the same as a trace-free kitchen. Ask about their protocol first.

What to order

Marinara or Margherita from the dining-room menu.

What might disappoint

A 4★ review describes a thin centre and thick crust and asks for more vegan options. Vegetarian is not vegan.

Before you go

Use the dining-room menu: the online delivery catalogue shows different prices.

What reviews say

Service and pizza or pasta well rated; vegans have little choice.
