Linaje

family restaurant, Central Market / Sant Francesc

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Families in the centre who want to eat while the child plays on site.

Not your plan if…

Assuming supervision all day: staff for children are only declared at weekends.

What to order

Valencian rice or meat and fish from the Josper charcoal oven, the signature of this Grupo Gómez restaurant.

What might disappoint

It is a big place (about 180 seats), not a quiet corner; and whether you can see the children’s room from your table depends on where you sit.

Before you go

Childcare staff are there at weekends only. When you book, ask for a table near the children’s room and check ages and cost.

What’s on offer

Mediterranean food, rice dishes and a children's room.

Dessert here or out?

For something sweet, head to La Romana San Vicente or Véneta Ribera, in the same area.

Check the sweet stop’s hours for the time you actually leave; the ones that close early won’t work after dinner.

Alquería del Brosquil

rice restaurant in a farmhouse, Castellar-Oliveral

Documentary research

Rice in a farmhouse among market gardens: a long lunch worth the trip, not a stop on a stroll.

The catch: More than two different rice dishes per table: the menu limits it to two.

In common: rice & paella; in another area (Malvarrosa / seafront promenade); check each menu.

Casa Carmela

rice restaurant, Malvarrosa / seafront promenade

Documentary research

Wood-fired rice in Malvarrosa, for a rice lunch where you don’t hold back.

The catch: A cheap meal, or an impromptu paella valenciana: it needs ordering 24 hours ahead.
