Daniel Mercado de Colón

horchata bar, Eixample / Colón / Cánovas

Documentary researchGood value

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

A Valencian stop after lunch or for an afternoon snack: horchata and fartons without leaving the Mercado de Colón.

Not your plan if…

Ending a late dinner: it closes at 22:00 according to Mercado de Colón.

Our labels

  • Good value Reviews highlight good quality at a fair price. (gastroranking.es, review due by 24 December 2026)

What to order

Horchata with fartons (there are chocolate-filled and gluten-free ones); for an extra treat, the flotador, horchata with ice cream.

What might disappoint

At weekends there is a queue for tables; one review mentions more than half an hour.

Before you go

If there is a queue, get it to take away. It closes at 22:00, according to the Mercado de Colón.

What reviews say

Quality horchata at a fair price; at weekends, be patient.
