La Principal

restaurant, Mestalla / Alameda

Documentary research

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Produce, a bar counter and rice by the portion in Mestalla; private rooms for groups too.

Not your plan if…

A cheap meal: this is a produce-led place, and the bill shows it.

What to order

Rice per portion, minimum two: arroz a banda €16, paella valenciana to pre-order €17. Ask the price of off-menu specials.

What might disappoint

It isn’t cheap: the Macarfi guide puts it at around €60 per person without drinks.

Before you go

It has private rooms, good for groups and work dinners: book. Don’t confuse kitchen closing time with the dining room’s.

What reviews say

Fresh produce and private rooms for groups; not a cheap meal.
