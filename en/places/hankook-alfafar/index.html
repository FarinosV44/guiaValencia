Hankook Alfafar

Korean barbecue buffet, Alfafar (outside Valencia city)

Documentary researchOutside Valencia city

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

Who it suits

Hungry groups who enjoy grilling in rounds: tabletop grill plus sushi, at a buffet price.

Not your plan if…

Light eaters, or anyone wanting one specific dish: unlimited access adds little.

What to order

Choose basic or premium knowing the basic tier charges premium supplements.

What might disappoint

A children’s price does not make it right for a child who keeps getting up: it is tabletop grilling, with flames and rounds.

Before you go

Drinks and dessert extra. The children’s price goes by height: ask when you book. There is a Hankook in Barcelona: its prices do not apply here.

What’s on offer

Grill-and-sushi buffet, outside Valencia city.

Children and families

Cost: Children’s price goes by height: ask for the bands when you book.
