Another Neapolitan pizzeria in Valencia? Fine, but tell me why

The crust now has more volume than many flats. Fine. Now we want to know what is in the middle, what it costs and whether we can sit down before we lose the will to talk.

This shortlist starts from three names suggested by a reader and checks their menus. It is not a comparative tasting of all Valencia, and the suggestion is not an editorial award.

Pick the difference that matters to you

If a focused menu matters most and you are up to four, start with Raíces. For mixed tastes in Ruzafa, Filippa’s. For vegan options and local produce in Benimaclet, Km0. We’ll leave the best-dough podium for when we’ve eaten them under comparable conditions.

Raíces

pizzeria, El Carmen / La Seu

Documentary research

Contemporary pizza from a short menu by the Cathedral, for groups of two to four who don’t mind waiting for a table.

The catch: Needing a guaranteed seating time: no bookings.

€19 per person, partial total

Filippa’s

pizzeria, Ruzafa

Documentary research

A table of mixed tastes in Ruzafa: Neapolitan pizza for some, pasta for others.

The catch: Strict coeliacs: having gluten-free options is not the same as a trace-free kitchen. Ask about their protocol first.

€11.50 per person, base price

Pizzería Km0

pizzeria, Benimaclet

Documentary researchGood value

Neapolitan pizza on 48-hour dough with local produce, and the best vegan section on our list, in Benimaclet.

The catch: Anyone who only wants the usual Margherita: the seasonal pizzas are the point here.

€10.50 per person, base price

Do we stay for dessert?

A pizzeria does not oblige you to flee the tiramisu, nor to order it. Raíces lists its own desserts so you can budget them; to say they are especially worth it we would need to taste them. In Ruzafa you could stroll to Antigua Lechería, checking opening and flavours of the day. A stroll is not an automatic second bill.

A simple pizza helps compare dough and bake; a special shows how the kitchen handles toppings. Pistachio can come to dinner: it doesn’t need to chair every meeting.
