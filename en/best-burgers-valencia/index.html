Burgers in Valencia: cheddar is not a strategy

The burger has gone from having dinner with you to needing an agent. It comes with an award, a limited edition, an Anglo-Saxon surname and a sauce that may have a longer biography than you. We can enjoy it and still read the bill.

A starting order, with reasons

An outside award, our own tasting and fit for your plan are three different things. This order combines the outside reference (Burgerdudes 2026, not Michelin) with what suits each plan; it is not our taste score.

Hundred Burgers

burger restaurant, Central Market / Sant Francesc

Documentary research

Anyone who wants to try the burger Burgerdudes named the world’s best: 100 seats and plenty of demand.

The catch: Anyone after a quiet, hidden burger joint: this is the most famous one in town.

Soul Coffee Beer

burger restaurant

Documentary researchSeveral branches

Burger-list chasers: Soul has made Burgerdudes’ world top 10.

The catch: A coffee stop because of the name: it is a burger restaurant.

Tasty El Carmen

burger restaurant, El Carmen / La Seu

Documentary research

A cheap, casual smash burger in El Carmen: reviews rank it among the best smash burgers in Valencia.

The catch: A play area: a children's menu does not make it one.

€12.50 per person, partial total

Beak & Trotter

burger restaurant, Cabanyal / Canyamelar

Documentary research

Hungry friends who want to share ribs and smoky burgers; reviews highlight generous portions and staff who give good recommendations.

The catch: An intimate dinner: a small room that is nearly always full.

How our own ranking should be decided

Judging the cooking takes comparable visits with receipts: a bun that holds up, meat cooked as ordered, balanced sauce, temperature and consistency. For value we add burger, fries when not included, drink and supplements. A starter does not become compulsory just because it is named like a rapper.

On a relaxed date you want to talk without being rushed. With active children, waiting time and space matter, not whether there is a small burger. Winning a championship certifies none of that.
