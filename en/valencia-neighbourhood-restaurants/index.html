Eating well off the beaten track: talent can park outside Ruzafa too

Good cooking doesn’t need a fashionable postcode. Nor do we need to call a place ‘hidden’ when half the neighbourhood knows it. Here we look for reasons to make the trip, and say when it isn’t worth it for your plan.

Castellar-Oliveral

El Baret de la Tòfona

bar-restaurant, Castellar-Oliveral

Documentary research

Rice and market cooking in Castellar, worth a deliberate trip with time to spare.

The catch: A cheap neighbourhood set menu: a recent review found no set menu and recalls around €40–50 per person.

Alquería del Brosquil

rice restaurant in a farmhouse, Castellar-Oliveral

Documentary research

Rice in a farmhouse among market gardens: a long lunch worth the trip, not a stop on a stroll.

The catch: More than two different rice dishes per table: the menu limits it to two.

La Petxina / Extramurs

Neither remote outskirts nor a newly discovered secret; it is here for its produce-led cooking and counter classics.

Bar Ricardo

bar, La Petxina / Extramurs

Documentary research

Classic neighbourhood tapas with serious produce in La Petxina: bravas, ensaladilla and cuttlefish.

The catch: Ordering seafood by weight without asking: the bill moves into another league.

€15.20 per person, partial total

Torrefiel: next candidate, not a firm recommendation

Malkebien

restaurant, Torrefiel

Documentary researchGood value

A weekday lunch in Torrefiel: a home-style set menu with lots of vegetables, quick and good value, with a loyal local crowd.

The catch: Dinner: its opening table ends at 17:00, even though the website promotes dinners. Call before going in the evening.

Questions worth the trip

Is the detour worth it? If you are going for one particular place, yes; if you are just passing nearby, pick something in your own area. Book, check the day’s set menu on their website and plan the way back in Maps before you set off.
