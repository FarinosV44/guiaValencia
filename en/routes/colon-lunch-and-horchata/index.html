Colón: lunch at Habitual and horchata in the same market

Habitual and Daniel share Mercado de Colón: lunch and horchata without crossing the city. La Romana on Joaquín Costa would be a third sweet stop, so it stays out of the plan.

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

  1. Habitual

    The long menu brings together well-known Camarena dishes; the Michelin Guide mentions the Galician beef tartare with egg-yolk foam, the organic tomato tatin and fried eggs with lobster and sobrasada.

  2. Stroll

    From the lower floor up to the market floor, inside the same building. With a pushchair, ask about the lift.

  3. Daniel Mercado de Colón optional

    Horchata with fartons (there are chocolate-filled and gluten-free ones); for an extra treat, the flotador, horchata with ice cream.
