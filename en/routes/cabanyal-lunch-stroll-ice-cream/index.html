Cabanyal: lunch at Anyora, a stroll, then decide where dessert happens

An early lunch at Anyora and a stroll through Cabanyal's streets to Gelática. Or stay for Anyora's cremaet ice cream and skip the ice-cream shop. One dessert, not two.

Printed from Where to eat in Valencia. Data checked on 24 October 2026: confirm hours and prices before you go.

  1. Anyora

    To share: tripe (callos), anchovies (boquerones) and cuttlefish (sepia). For dessert, the cremaet ice cream has Valencian character. Reviews mention aubergine with cheese.

  2. Stroll

    A stroll through Cabanyal's streets. Time and route: open it in Maps; we have not measured it or checked crossings or pushchair access.

    Open the stroll in Maps

  3. Gelática Ice Concept optional

    Reviews mention chocolate with turrón, lemon sorbet, pistachio and brownie. A single scoop is not the only reason to go.
