Routes: eat, stroll and decide where dessert happens

Lunch, stroll and dessert plans. The full route is checked against opening hours; the others are ideas to build your day around.

Complete sample route

Cabanyal: lunch at Anyora, a stroll, then decide where dessert happens

An early lunch at Anyora and a stroll through Cabanyal's streets to Gelática. Or stay for Anyora's cremaet ice cream and skip the ice-cream shop. One dessert, not two.

Colón: lunch at Habitual and horchata in the same market

Habitual and Daniel share Mercado de Colón: lunch and horchata without crossing the city. La Romana on Joaquín Costa would be a third sweet stop, so it stays out of the plan.

Cabanyal: lunch at La Sastrería, then dessert inside or at Gelática

A daytime route: lunch at La Sastrería (Thursday to Sunday), a stroll and Gelática if you want dessert out. Casa Montaña is the bodega alternative; book. At night, stay for dessert.

More route ideas

Combinations that work by distance and style. Check opening hours in Maps before you set off.

  • Carmen: set lunch and a stroll, without paying for two dessertsBlanqueries, Véneta BrodadorsSet menu at Blanqueries, which already includes dessert; Véneta Brodadors only if you swap that dessert for ice cream, not to add one.
  • Carmen: tapas on Mossén Sorell or Greek foodLa Rentaora, Kuzina, Véneta BrodadorsTwo ways to eat in El Carmen: tapas and wine at La Rentaora or Greek at Kuzina. Ice cream at Véneta Brodadors; after Kuzina’s baklava you may not need it.
  • Centre: market bite and something sweetCentral Bar, La Romana San VicenteA morning or lunchtime plan: Central Bar keeps Mercado Central hours and is closed on Sundays and holidays. Afterwards, ice cream at La Romana on Sant Vicent.
  • Ruzafa: coffee first, food laterBluebell Coffee, Canalla Bistro, Antigua LecheríaSpecialty coffee at Bluebell in the morning, a sharing lunch at Canalla and inventive ice cream at Antigua Lechería, closed on Mondays. If Canalla’s menu includes dessert, pick one.
  • Ruzafa with an active child: one meal and playCanalla Bistro, HippolatteLunch at Canalla and play at Hippolatte, designed for 3-to-5-year-olds. Book a Hippolatte session before you sit down to eat.
  • Mestalla-Alameda: eat nearby and playLa Principal, Komori, Petit NiuEat at La Principal or Komori and spend the afternoon playing at Petit Niu (ages 0–6); ask there about the entry price when you book.
  • Benimaclet: travelling flavours and horchataLa Negri, Els Sariers, La Ola FrescaLunch at La Negri, with its cheesecake for dessert, or at La Ola Fresca; then horchata at Els Sariers, which opens in the afternoon on weekdays.
  • Seafront: rice and ice cream without crossing the cityLa Pepica, Véneta NeptunoRice at La Pepica (allow 40–45 minutes’ wait) and ice cream at Véneta Neptuno, on the same promenade.
  • Zaidía: eat and play without doubling the billGato BlancoEat and play in one place: Gato Blanco has a children’s area, so there is no need for another stop or another bill.
