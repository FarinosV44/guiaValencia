Tapas in Valencia: decide whether you’re here for dinner or a snack

Tapas are deceptive: three plates look small and five with wine are already dinner. We separate places to make a proper meal of tapas from those for a quick bite before strolling on.

To make a meal of it

Bar Ricardo for classics with serious produce (bravas, ensaladilla, cuttlefish); Anyora for stews and seafood cooking; Casa Montaña for brandada and the bodega; La Principal for counter dining and produce in Mestalla. Watch seafood sold by weight: it changes the bill’s league.

Bar Ricardo

bar, La Petxina / Extramurs

Documentary research

Classic neighbourhood tapas with serious produce in La Petxina: bravas, ensaladilla and cuttlefish.

The catch: Ordering seafood by weight without asking: the bill moves into another league.

€15.20 per person, partial total

Anyora

bodega restaurant, Cabanyal / Canyamelar

Documentary research

Valencian food chosen for culinary reasons, not just the décor.

The catch: Sundays: the kitchen is closed according to its website.

€19.50 per person, partial total

Casa Montaña

bodega restaurant, Cabanyal / Canyamelar

Documentary research

A classic Cabanyal bodega: brandada, tapas and wine, if you know what to order.

The catch: Arriving late and expecting to linger: one review mentions being rushed after arriving late.

€50 per person, partial total

La Principal

restaurant, Mestalla / Alameda

Documentary research

Produce, a bar counter and rice by the portion in Mestalla; private rooms for groups too.

The catch: A cheap meal: this is a produce-led place, and the bill shows it.

€17 per person, partial total

To snack and stroll on

La Rentaora for cheese, charcuterie and wine in El Carmen; Central Bar for the Mercado Central counter at lunchtime; La Negri for globe-trotting neighbourhood food in Benimaclet.

La Rentaora

tavern, El Carmen / La Seu

Documentary research

Snacks and wine on Mossén Sorell: cheese, charcuterie, then on with your stroll through El Carmen.

The catch: A full dinner when you are properly hungry: this is grazing.

Central Bar

bar, Central Market / Sant Francesc

Documentary research

Ricard Camarena’s sandwich at the Mercado Central counter: a market esmorzar or tapas at lunchtime.

The catch: Dinner: the kitchen closes at 15:00 (15:30 on Friday and Saturday).

€7.50 per person, partial total

La Negri

bar-restaurant, Benimaclet

Documentary research

Globe-trotting neighbourhood cooking in Benimaclet, with a cheesecake that has a reputation, and a terrace.

The catch: Mondays and Tuesdays: closed according to its website.

€15 per person, base price
